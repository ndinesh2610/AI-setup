# Insurance (Policy)

Purpose: this policy explains the rule on insurance.

Rule: Parcels are covered up to a value of 500 unless extra cover is bought.

Scope: it applies to everyone who works with the parcel logistics service.

Exceptions: an exception needs approval from the customer.
