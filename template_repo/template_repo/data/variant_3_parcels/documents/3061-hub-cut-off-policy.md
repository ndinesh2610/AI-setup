# Hub cut-off (Policy)

Purpose: this policy explains the rule on hub cut-off.

Rule: Parcels handed in after 17:00 are scanned the next working day.

Scope: it applies to everyone who works with the parcel logistics service.

Exceptions: an exception needs approval from the customer.
