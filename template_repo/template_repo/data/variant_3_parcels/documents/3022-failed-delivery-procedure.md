# Failed delivery (Procedure)

Follow these steps.

1. Check who is asking and that they may see this information.
2. Apply the rule: After a failed delivery the driver tries again on the next working day, up to 2 more times.
3. Write down what you decided and why.
4. If the case is unusual, ask the customer.
