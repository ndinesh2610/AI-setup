# Failed delivery (Policy)

Purpose: this policy explains the rule on failed delivery.

Rule: After a failed delivery the driver tries again on the next working day, up to 2 more times.

Scope: it applies to everyone who works with the parcel logistics service.

Exceptions: an exception needs approval from the customer.
