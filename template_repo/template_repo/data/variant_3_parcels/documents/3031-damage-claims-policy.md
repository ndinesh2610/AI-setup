# Damage claims (Policy)

Purpose: this policy explains the rule on damage claims.

Rule: A damage claim must be filed within 14 days of delivery with photos.

Scope: it applies to everyone who works with the parcel logistics service.

Exceptions: an exception needs approval from the customer.
